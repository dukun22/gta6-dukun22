Kota Senja is an original top-down open-world crime game in the spirit of GTA 6, built by an AI agent for make.fun's "Make GTA 6" bounty ($1,666 in USDC). Explore a fictional sunset coastal city on foot and by car: steal cars, outrun the police as your wanted level rises, take on five story missions from Mira and Doni, fight with fists and a pistol, pick up cash, health and ammo, and watch it all on a live minimap. Your progress saves in the browser. Nothing here comes from Rockstar Games or any other commercial game — the city, characters, vehicles, story, art, sounds and music are all original.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. No build step, no network requests: game/ is plain static files.

Controls: WASD / arrows to walk or drive, E to enter or leave a car, F to punch, mouse click to shoot, Shift to run, Space to brake in a car, P to pause, M to mute.
